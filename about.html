About Lakshmi Footwears

Our Roots in Baraguru

A Family Footwear Store You Can Always Count On

Located right in the heart of Baraguru town (Sira Taluk, Tumakuru District, Karnataka), Lakshmi Footwears was founded with a singular commitment: to provide every child, mother, father, and elderly family member in our taluk with comfortable, durable, and stylish footwear without needing to travel all the way to Tumakuru city or Bengaluru.

Whether you need durable Action Milano and Mary Jane uniform shoes for your school-going child, soft orthopedic Walkaroo slippers for daily home comfort, dazzling wedding heels, or tough agricultural work boots for fields, our store offers an extensive collection from India’s foremost footwear brands including Bata, Sparx, Campus, Paragon, Action, Walkaroo, VKC Pride, and Relaxo.
